# Video Prompt: Laundry Pickup Service (6 seconds)

## Video Description
Create a 6-second video showing a professional laundry service provider picking up clothes from a customer's location.

## Video Specifications
- **Duration**: Exactly 6 seconds
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (1920x1080) or 9:16 (1080x1920) for vertical format
- **Frame Rate**: 24fps or 30fps
- **Style**: Professional, clean, modern, trustworthy
- **Mood**: Friendly, efficient, reliable

## Scene Description

### Opening (0-2 seconds)
- A professional laundry service vehicle (clean van or car with FreshFold branding/logo) approaches and parks in front of a modern residential home
- The vehicle stops near the entrance (driveway or street parking)
- Setting: Modern residential area - suburban home or urban neighborhood
- Lighting: Natural daylight, bright and welcoming
- Camera angle: Wide shot showing the vehicle arriving, then medium shot of vehicle parked

### Middle (2-4 seconds)
- A male professional laundry service provider (in clean, branded uniform) exits the vehicle
- The provider carries a professional laundry bag or basket
- Provider approaches the residential door
- Provider rings the doorbell or knocks
- A male customer opens the door
- Customer hands over a bag or basket of clothes to the provider
- Brief, professional exchange - nod of acknowledgment
- Camera: Medium shot capturing the handoff moment, showing both provider and customer

### Closing (4-6 seconds)
- The provider carefully receives the laundry bag/basket
- Provider places the laundry securely in their professional collection bag/basket
- Provider returns to the vehicle and places the laundry inside
- Provider gets into the vehicle and drives away toward the laundry facility
- Camera: Medium shot showing provider loading laundry into vehicle, then wide shot of vehicle driving away

## Visual Elements

### Characters
- **Provider**: 
  - Male professional (no women)
  - Professional appearance (clean, neat uniform with FreshFold branding)
  - Friendly, approachable demeanor
  - Carries professional laundry collection equipment (bag, basket)
  - Has a mobile device or tablet for order management (optional)

- **Customer**: 
  - Male customer (no women)
  - Casual, comfortable appearance
  - Friendly, welcoming expression
  - Hands over laundry items in a bag or basket

- **Vehicle**:
  - Professional laundry service vehicle (van or car)
  - Clean, well-maintained appearance
  - May have FreshFold branding/logo visible
  - Modern, professional vehicle suitable for laundry service

### Setting
- Modern residential location (apartment building, suburban home, or townhouse)
- Clean, well-maintained exterior
- Good lighting (daylight preferred)
- Professional, trustworthy environment

### Props
- Professional laundry service vehicle (van or car with branding)
- Laundry bag or basket (clean, professional-looking)
- Mobile device/tablet for order confirmation (optional)
- Customer's laundry items (folded clothes visible in bag/basket)
- Professional collection bag/basket for the provider
- Vehicle interior showing organized laundry storage

## Technical Requirements

### Camera Movement
- Smooth, steady camera work
- No shaky footage
- Professional cinematography
- May include slight dolly or tracking movement for dynamic feel

### Color Palette
- Clean, bright colors
- Professional blues, whites, and grays (FreshFold brand colors if applicable)
- Natural, warm lighting
- High contrast for clarity

### Audio (if needed)
- Ambient sounds: gentle doorbell, footsteps, light conversation
- No background music (or very subtle, professional music)
- Natural, authentic soundscape

## Brand Integration
- Provider may wear FreshFold-branded clothing or accessories
- Professional, modern aesthetic matching FreshFold brand identity
- Clean, efficient service representation
- Trustworthy, reliable appearance

## Use Case
This video will be used to illustrate the pickup process in the FreshFold laundry service platform, showing customers how easy and professional the service pickup experience is.

## Alternative Angles/Shots
- Option 1: Vehicle arriving and parking at residential home
- Option 2: Provider exiting vehicle and approaching door
- Option 3: Close-up of handoff moment (customer to provider)
- Option 4: Provider loading laundry into vehicle
- Option 5: Vehicle driving away toward laundry facility

## Key Message
"Professional, reliable, and friendly laundry pickup service - FreshFold makes it easy."
